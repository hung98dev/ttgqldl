# M00-W0002 — Numerical and temporal reference vector catalog

## Deliverable
`tooling/refeval` is a second, independent implementation of the documented equations in 02_GAME_RULES.md and 03_SIMULATION.md. It is deliberately NOT production code: M02 owns the authoritative reducer and must consume these vectors without importing this program.

- 23 curated cases with full per-boundary traces,
- a 1080-run population/role/difficulty sweep with a stable SHA-256 digest,
- 100+ hard assertions, all hand-derived from the formulas before running the code,
- `tooling/refeval/testdata/golden_vectors.json` as the machine-consumable artifact for M02 (211,206 bytes).

## Method
For each vector the expected values were computed by hand from the documented formulas (enemy HP, lane split, subpool split, builder generation, repair cost, construction work, wall upgrade, attack rotation, retaliation, guard mitigation, settlement) and only then compared with the evaluator. Where hand computation and evaluator disagreed, the evaluator was re-inspected; the disagreements found were traced to incomplete hand arithmetic for repair ordering, and the model was checked line by line against 02 step order (1)–(7).

## The two QA-BAL-03 vectors (hand-derived, then confirmed)

**C001 — P=10, B2/G2/F6, wave 5 boss, d=1, clean village**
- base_hp 162 → west 55 / center 51 / east 56; subpools runner/grunt/brute by 25/55/remainder.
- attack per combat boundary 40 = 18*isqrt(6) + militia(40-4*(10-1)) = 36 + 4.
- guard block 8; retaliation 31 → damage 23 at t=1800.
- The boss pool (33 HP) is created at tick2600 and dies at tick2600 through redistributed damage; ordinary pools reach 0 at tick2600.
- Settlement at tick2800: victory, wall 1000, wood 518, stone 324, food 350, session XP 100, tier 1.

**C002 — P=30, B8/G8/F14, wave 5 boss, d=1, clean village**
- base_hp 238 → 80/76/82; boss 48; attack 54 = 18*isqrt(14); guard block 16.
- At tick2600 the boss survives with **16 HP**; the tick2800 combat kills it.
- Settlement: victory, wall 1000, wood 544, stone 338, session XP 300, tier 1.
- This vector is the mandatory regression for QA-SIM-10: an implementation that excludes tick2800 from combat settles a LOSS on this identical state.

## Measured balance characteristics (recorded, not silently retuned)
Minimum fighters to clear a boss wave at d=1, no prior losses, no vote:

| P | 1 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 10 | 30 | 100 | 1000 | 10000 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| min fighters | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 4 | 9 | 25 | 196 | 1849 |
| share | 0% | 0% | 0% | 20% | 40% | 30% | 25% | 19.6% | 18.5% |

The curve is NON-monotonic in population: the militia falls off faster than enemy HP rises, so P=10 demands a higher fighter share than P=10000. Two further measured facts: at 0% fighters a wave is lost for P>=5 but won for P<=4; at 25% fighters 5/24 baseline runs win at P=10 and 8/24 at P=30. These are documented as accepted difficulty characteristics in 02_GAME_RULES.md; **no coefficient was changed** to make any test pass.

## Defects found and fixed in the owning modules
- **M00-A04 (BLOCKER)**: the attack-remainder order and whether the boss is a legal redistribution target were ambiguous. Round-2 redistribution including the boss is what kills the C002 boss at tick2800; the opposite reading changes authored outcomes. 02 now specifies the four-part algorithm exactly.
- **M00-A02 (BLOCKER)**: XP had no place in the tick order. 03 now awards XP inside the boundary, before economy and before the defeat check.
- **M00-A05 (BLOCKER)**: session XP was absent from the canonical hash, so a duplicated-XP crash could hash identically. 04 now hashes per-member `session_xp`.
- **M00-A12 (BLOCKER)**: the boss pool must not exist before tick2600; `boss_hp=0`, `boss_spawned=false` are now explicit and hashed.
- **M00-A15 (MEDIUM)**: QA-BAL-03's "win at tick2800" wording contradicted the measured P=10 kill at 2600; both vectors are now stated with exact values.
- **M00-A16 (MEDIUM)**: the measured fighter-share curve is recorded instead of inviting an undocumented balance edit.
