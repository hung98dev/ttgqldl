## 5. What the client may display

- `Ttgqldl.Core` holds an immutable `PublicSnapshot` mirroring the normative
  `public_snapshot` projection (schema version 1, wave, lifecycle, phase,
  remaining seconds, wall/townhall HP, wood/stone/food, builder/guard/fighter
  counts, three ordered lanes, vote, source status, spotlights, banner,
  `boss_incoming`).
- `PublicStateValidator` refuses null, unsupported schema, out-of-range HP,
  resources, timers, lane count/order, vote options, more than 12 spotlights,
  unknown roles, empty banner keys, `boss_incoming` during RESULTS, and any
  `public_ref` shaped like a 64-hex actor key.
- `PresentationMapper` produces a render-only `HudViewModel`; it never computes
  resources, combat, XP or victory. `ClearVisible` is false while
  `boss_incoming`, and `VictoryVisible` requires the published RESULTS phase.
- The runtime keeps the last accepted view when a later snapshot is invalid and
  marks it STALE; it never displays unvalidated data.
- `OfflineFixtureSource` is deterministic, I/O-free and always carries
  `SourceStatus.Mock`, so the persistent `MOCK — OFFLINE FIXTURE` watermark is on.
- `IPublicStateStreamAdapter` is the isolated M05 boundary: no endpoint, URL or
  token handling exists yet and nothing connects to TikTok.
