## Population anchor

At tick 1600 the anchor locks once from unique current-wave members with
`joined_tick <= 1599`:

- joining at 1599 counts; joining at 1600 is excluded;
- duplicates and role switches never increase the count;
- `ActualPopulation` stays truthful; formulas use
  `P = max(1, min(actual, 10000))`;
- no fictitious viewer is created for the mathematical minimum of 1;
- the lock is idempotent and never recalculates.

## Difficulty and base HP

`d = min(10, 1 + consecutive_wins)`; recovery modifier
`100 - 15*min(consecutive_losses,3)` = 100/85/70/55.

```
base_hp = max(1, floor((40 + 8*d + 38*isqrt(P)) * recovery / 100))
```

Reference values verified against the independent derivation:

| d | P | base_hp |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 86 |
| 1 | 3 | 86 |
| 1 | 4 | 124 |
| 1 | 10 | 162 |
| 4 | 10 | 186 |
| 10 | 100 | 500 |

## Lane and subpool conservation

`west = floor(base*34/100)`, `center = floor(base*32/100)`, `east = remainder`;
per lane `runner = floor(lane*25/100)`, `grunt = floor(lane*55/100)`,
`brute = remainder`. Pools are exactly nine HP counters ordered
west/center/east then runner/grunt/brute, all non-negative, and their sum equals
`base_hp` at lock time. Tests assert conservation for bases 1..1,000,000 and that
depleted pools remain valid (combat only reduces HP).

Militia is system assistance: no member, no XP, no role count, no anchor change,
no fake viewer. W0204 derives the value only; applying it is W0205.

## Boss state

- boss waves are `wave_no % 5 == 0`;
- at tick 1600 `boss_max_hp = ceil(base_hp/5)` is derived but no boss exists;
- before 2600 `boss_hp = 0`, `boss_spawned = false`;
- at 2600 the boss spawns once with HP = maximum, ordinary pools preserved, and
  `boss_spawned` is emitted after the `boss_spawn` boundary and before `combat`;
- non-boss waves never derive or spawn a boss;
- a spawned boss at exactly 0 HP stays valid until rollover;
- `BOSS_INCOMING` is reported when ordinary pools are empty before the spawn, and
  is never reported as CLEAR; no victory is produced before settlement.
